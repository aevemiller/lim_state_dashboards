Field insights

Two questions, and the data hands you the talking points. The quoted lines are ready to use word-for-word if they help — or just take the numbers and make them your own. Anything marked “for you” or “Internal” is prep, not wording to share.

Insights regenerate from the data every time you change the district, so they stay current with each data refresh. Wording distinguishes significant results from descriptive ones on purpose: descriptive is fine to say, it just cannot be called proven.
